About

A calm, human hand through processes that feel intimidating.

TechVisaAssist is a B2C IT consultancy run by Elias Lenz. We help individuals navigate German e-government services and guided visa and residence-permit applications — ELSTER, Bürgeramt online portals, visa portals and document preparation. No jargon, no pressure: just clear, patient guidance at your pace.

  • Multilingual support

    Sessions in English and German, with Kiswahili on request.

  • GDPR-compliant

    Your documents and data are handled securely and confidentially, the EU way.

  • Experienced IT consultant

    Years of hands-on experience with digital public services and online portals.

  • Secure video calls

    Private, encrypted video sessions — help you can get from home.

Confirmation & cancellation policy
