Engine 1 — Facilitation

DigiAssist delivers eight workshops and four programmes built on CyberSec Games decks (Elevation of Privilege, OWASP Cornucopia and Cumulus, LINDDUN GO, PLOT4ai, Backdoors & Breaches, Byte Club). Every session ends with a document — threat model, backlog, AI risk register or a NIS2 training record.

Engine 2 — Partner store

Your catalogue on the TechVisaAssist learning store with a Kestura partner code. CyberSec Games remains seller of record and ships directly; Kestura earns a referral commission and buys partner-priced decks for workshop bundles.

Proposed terms

Non-exclusive, 12 months, no purchase minimums in year one, licence for marketing assets, mutual partner listing, assignment to affiliates (dedicated TechVisaAssist company planned for 2027). Target: signature by 31 October, go-live in November.
